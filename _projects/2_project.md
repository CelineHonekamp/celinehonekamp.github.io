# Feedback for Motor Control
When we move around our environment, there is a constant stream of different sensory information coming in. From hearing, smelling and seeing "feeling" what your body is doing (so-called proprioception) is one very important source of information.

Amputees with prosthetics miss this important source since the cool mechanical devices cannot yet provide them.

Substituting this stream with other modalities or providing information at different locations on the body might provide a useful way of restoring normal function.
